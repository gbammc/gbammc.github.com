Zone 隐私政策

更新日期：2026年10月18日

Zone 是一款专注计时与白噪音应用。应用无需注册开发者账户，我们不运营用于收集你的专注记录或使用行为的服务器。

本机数据

应用在设备上保存专注记录（例如开始时间、时长和完成情况）、计时设置、语言选择及白噪音偏好。白噪音音频随应用提供。应用没有广告、第三方行为分析或跨应用追踪功能。

可选的 iCloud 同步

开启 iCloud 同步后，专注记录通过 Apple CloudKit 保存到你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库，并在使用同一账户的设备间同步。此过程由 Apple 提供服务，受 Apple 的隐私政策及 iCloud 条款约束。开发者不会通过开发者自建服务器接收这些记录。你可以在应用设置中关闭同步；关闭同步不会自动删除已有云端数据。

通知、音频与权限

通知权限用于专注和休息结束提醒，你可以拒绝或在系统设置中关闭。后台音频用于继续播放你选择的白噪音。应用不使用麦克风录音。

反馈与联系

如果你主动通过电子邮件发送反馈，收件人会收到你的邮件地址及你选择提供的内容，用于处理反馈。请勿在反馈中提供不必要的敏感信息。如需咨询隐私或数据处理问题，请联系 zonetimerapp@gmail.com。

数据管理与政策更新

卸载应用可移除设备上的应用数据，但不代表删除 iCloud 或设备备份中的数据；这些数据应通过 Apple 提供的账户及存储管理功能处理。如应用的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页。

Zone Privacy Policy

Updated October 18, 2026

Zone is a focus timer and ambient-audio app. No developer account is required. We do not operate a server to collect your focus records or usage behavior.

On-device data

Zone stores focus records, such as start times, duration and completion state, together with timer settings, language selection and audio preferences on your device. Audio files are bundled with the app. Zone has no advertising, third-party behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking.

Optional iCloud sync

When enabled, focus records are stored in your own private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit and synchronized between devices using the same account. Apple provides this service under its privacy policy and iCloud terms. We do not receive these records through a developer-operated server. You can disable sync in the app; doing so does not automatically delete existing cloud data.

Notifications and audio

Notifications provide focus and break reminders. You can decline permission or disable it in system settings. Background audio continues playing the ambient sound you select. Zone does not record microphone audio.

Feedback and contact

If you send email feedback, the recipient receives your email address and the content you choose to include so the feedback can be addressed. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information. For privacy questions, contact zonetimerapp@gmail.com.

Managing data and policy updates

Uninstalling removes app data from the device, but does not delete iCloud data or backups. Manage those through Apple's account and storage controls. We will update this page if the app's data practices change.
